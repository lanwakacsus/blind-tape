    About this game

    Blind Tape is a trading game I built so I could practise without losing money. The market runs on its own clock, one trading day at a time, and you never see the next bar. You go long, short or flat. You choose how big the position is, and whether a stop-loss or a profit target closes it for you. Every trade costs 0.10%, the same as the bots pay.

    Six bots trade the same tape as you: buy and hold, a moving-average cross, momentum, a breakout, and two mean-reversion rules. After each round you can open the backtest lab, change their settings, sweep the parameters, and check them on 100 markets they have never seen. Please do that part. It is the reason I wrote this.

    There is a seventh racer, M. It is the bot I used while I was building the game. I never turned it off. It has been improving. I have not touched the code.

    Earn stars by finishing in profit, by beating buy and hold, and by finishing first. One star opens the next market. The daily tape is the same for everyone and changes at 00:00 UTC. Your first run counts.

    I would like to meet everyone who plays this. If you play it, write to me: mhale@blindtape.example. Any hour is fine. I answer every email.

    — M. Hale, August 2010

              How the bots work
              
              
              Each bot is a rule backtested on this exact tape. It decides at each daily close using only past bars and pays the same 0.10% per trade. Change a bot's settings and the standings update. Your settings carry into your next market, so you build the opponents you race.
