    About this game

    Blind Tape is a trading game I built so I could practise without losing money. You never see the next bar. Go long, short or flat. Every trade costs 0.10%, the same as the bots pay.

    Six bots trade the tape with you. A seventh, M., I never turned off. One star opens the next market, and every market is named for something I own: the geobasket, the ramones, the kiss boots, the hoodie, the tecuatl.

    This is an opium game. The label does not exist yet. Play it at night, in black, with the opium records on and the volume low. Do not turn it up. Do not ask who is signed.

    I would like to meet everyone who plays this: mhale@blindtape.example. Any hour is fine. I answer every email. Please do not email me about the shoes.

    — M. Hale, August 2010

              How the bots work
              
              
              Each bot is a rule backtested on this exact tape. It decides at each daily close using only past bars and pays the same 0.10% per trade. Change a bot's settings and the standings update. Your settings carry into your next market, so you build the opponents you race.

              The check below is statistics; this one you play. Your settings are frozen as they are now, you trade a fresh market of the same difficulty, and the debrief puts each bot's result there beside the result on the tape you tuned it on.
